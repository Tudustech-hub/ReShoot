# ReShoot

**A game where turrets shoot different types of bouncy balls at you, like tennis, translucent, bowling and slime balls!**

Made in Godot, and also made at my first game jam!

[▶ Play online on itch.io](https://tudustech.itch.io/rebounce)

</div>

---

## Installation

1. Go to the repository page.
2. Press the green **Code** button.
3. Click **Download ZIP**.
4. Extract the ZIP and open the project in Godot.

## How to play online?

Play directly in your browser here: https://tudustech.itch.io/rebounce


## Controls!
 * WASD Or UP,DOWN,LEFT,RIGHT Arrow on your keyboard
 * ESC Or P to pause
 * Left Click to Shoot
 * Move Mouse around to AIM
